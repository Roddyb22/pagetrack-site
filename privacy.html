Privacy Policy

PageTrack: Book Tracker · Effective date: November 21, 2026

PageTrack (“the app”) is published by Roderick Bourey (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. The short version: we don’t collect your reading data, we don’t have a PageTrack account system, and we don’t operate a PageTrack server that stores your library.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or data-collection SDKs. We cannot see your books, notes, highlights, journal entries, photos, or reading progress. Because we never collect or hold an account, there is nothing for us to delete on a server if you stop using the app — just delete the app on your devices (and, if you turned on iCloud sync, you can also remove the app’s data from your iCloud account in Settings).

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — your book list, progress, notes, journal entries, and related items — is stored on your device using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData). It stays there until you delete it or delete the app.

If you have enabled device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), this data may be included in your device backup, which is controlled by you and Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

The home-screen widget reads a small amount of this on-device data through an App Group on your device so it can show your reading progress. That data does not leave your device for the widget.

iCloud sync (optional, PageTrack Premium)

If you subscribe to PageTrack Premium and turn on iCloud sync, the app uses Apple’s CloudKit and your own iCloud account to keep your library in sync across your devices. That data is stored in your iCloud account, not on a server we operate. We cannot see it. Sync is optional, and your use of iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Camera, barcodes and text recognition

You can use the camera to scan a book barcode or to capture text with on-device optical character recognition (OCR). Scanning and OCR run on your device. The app does not upload the camera image to us. If you search for a scanned ISBN or title, that search is sent only to Open Library, as described below.

Book search (Open Library)

When you search for a book or look up an ISBN, the app sends the search terms or ISBN to Open Library, a public book catalog that does not require an API key from us. Open Library may also provide cover images that the app displays. We do not receive a copy of those requests. Open Library’s use of any information it receives is governed by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional PageTrack Premium purchases and subscriptions (monthly, yearly, and lifetime) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple tells the app only which products are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock features, including the higher book limit and optional iCloud sync. The yearly subscription may include a 7-day free trial. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is available in the app. The free version lets you track a limited number of books and has no ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the PageTrack site issues page. There is not a separate PageTrack support email yet.
