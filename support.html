PageTrack Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email pagetracksupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within one to two days.

From the app: open Settings and tap Contact support. It adds your app version to the email so we can help faster.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Apple subscriptions or Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data? PageTrack has no account. Open Settings and tap Erase all my data to delete everything on this device and in your private iCloud copy.

A book wasn't found. Use the Write in tab to add it by hand, and you can add a cover photo.
